How it works

Three steps to reclaim your day

No willpower lectures. Just a simple loop: pick apps → get blocked → earn unlock.

1

Choose apps to lock

Pick Instagram, TikTok, games — whatever derails you. Set immediate lock or a free timed window.

2

Get gently blocked

When time’s up, a SweatLock nudge appears. You can’t open the app until you complete the challenge.

3

Workout or read → unlock

Camera pose detection counts real reps. Or read 3 PDF pages. Only that app unlocks — not everything.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Does it work in the background?

Yes. On iOS, Screen Time / DeviceActivity monitors usage and applies shields even if SweatLock isn’t open. On Android, an Accessibility service detects the locked app and shows the unlock overlay.

Can I unlock just one app?

Yes. Completing a workout or reading challenge unlocks only the app you were trying to open — not every locked app.

What if I need the app urgently?

Settings includes one emergency unlock per day for a limited free window. Use it wisely.

Is my camera data private?

Pose detection runs on-device. SweatLock does not upload your workout video to a server.

iOS company requirement?

Family Controls requires the proper Apple entitlements. If you’re distributing publicly, follow Apple’s Screen Time / Family Controls enrollment guidance for your developer account.
