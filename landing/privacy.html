Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026 · Applies to the SweatLock mobile app and website.

Short version: Workout camera pose data stays on your device. We do not sell your personal data. Blocked-app choices and progress are stored locally (and, if you enable optional cloud features later, under your control).

1. Who we are

SweatLock (“we”, “us”) provides an app that locks selected applications until you complete a physical activity (workout, steps) or a reading comprehension challenge. Contact: privacy@sweatlock.app.

2. Data we process

  • On-device only (default): selected apps to block, workout reps/type, step goals, reading PDF path, quiz results, streaks, theme, and unlock timers — stored in local storage (Hive) on your phone.
  • Camera: used only for real-time pose detection during a workout session. Frames are processed on-device via ML Kit. We do not upload workout video or photos to our servers.
  • Motion / steps: pedometer or Health-related step counts used only to verify a step unlock challenge.
  • PDF books: files you upload remain on device; text used to generate comprehension quizzes is processed on-device where possible.
  • iOS Screen Time / Family Controls: Apple manages authorization tokens; we cannot read the content of other apps—only apply restrictions you authorize.
  • Android Accessibility: used solely to detect when a blocked package is in the foreground and to show the unlock overlay. We do not read passwords or message contents.
  • Diagnostics (optional): crash logs or anonymous analytics only if you opt in via OS or in-app settings.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal data to advertisers.
  • We do not use your camera stream for advertising profiles.
  • We do not access the content inside TikTok, Instagram, or other locked apps.

4. Legal bases (EEA/UK)

Where GDPR applies: processing is based on contract (providing the app you requested), legitimate interests (security, abuse prevention), and consent where required (camera, notifications, optional analytics).

5. Retention

Local data remains until you clear app data, log out, or uninstall. Emergency unlock counters reset daily. You can delete the reading PDF and blocked-app list from Settings.

6. Children’s privacy

SweatLock is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). Do not use the app if you are under that age without parental guidance as required by local law.

7. Third parties

Platform services (Apple, Google) process device permissions under their policies. Music unlocks may open Spotify or YouTube Music under those apps’ terms.

8. Your rights

Depending on your region, you may request access, correction, deletion, or portability of personal data we hold. Email privacy@sweatlock.app. For purely on-device data, use in-app controls or uninstall.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be noted by updating the “Last updated” date and, where appropriate, an in-app notice.

10. Contact

Privacy questions: privacy@sweatlock.app
